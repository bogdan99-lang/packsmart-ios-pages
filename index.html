PackSmart

Smart packing lists for every trip — built from your trip dates, trip type, activities and travelers.

Available for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) and for Android (7.0 or later).

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Lists that fit the trip

Trip types, activities, luggage and trip length shape a ready-to-use packing list. Start typing a destination and pick from the suggestions, or type any place you like.

Multiple luggage

Add up to 6 pieces of luggage per trip (Backpack, Carry-On, Checked Medium or Checked Large), give them optional names and assign each item to a bag.

Photos on items

Attach a photo to a packing item with the camera or from your gallery. Photos stay on your device.

Every traveler

Personal items for each traveler, shared items once for the group.

Daily outfits

Plan what to wear day by day and see what still needs packing.

Templates

Start from predefined templates. Premium: save your own and unlock the specialist templates. Everyone can unlock one Premium template for free with Your PackSmart Gift.

Weather-aware

Premium: destination forecast with packing suggestions.

Share & pack together

Send a copy of your list by link, or (Premium) invite people to pack the same list in real time. Links work between Android and iPhone.

AI Trip Planner

Premium: describe your trip in a sentence and get a complete plan.

Widgets & reminders

Track progress from your home screen and get reminded before you leave.

English & Română
